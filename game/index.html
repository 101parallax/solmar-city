    How to Play

    Work seven story jobs for fixer Marco and wheelwoman Lena, build your REP, and own Solmar City.

    • WASD / arrows — move and drive
    • Shift — NOS boost in a car, sprint on foot
    • F — enter, exit, or steal a car
    • E — interact (missions, shops, taxi, race)
    • Space — handbrake
    • Mouse + left click — aim and shoot
    • 1 / 2 / 3 — switch weapon
    • R — change radio station
    • M — full map
    • P / Esc — pause

    Stars mean the police are hunting you. Lose them, pay for a respray, or outrun the helicopter. Collect all 20 Sun Shells to reach 100% completion.
