SOLMAR CITY

A sun-soaked city. Your rules.
Rico Vane owes the wrong people money. Steal cars, outrun the Solmar PD, and run jobs for Marco Salas to pay it off — or take the city for yourself.
An original game — no real cars, brands or places. Every screenshot is a frame of it being played.

HOW TO PLAY

WASD / Arrows Walk · drive when in a car
Shift Sprint on foot
Space Handbrake / drift
F Enter / steal the nearest car
Mouse Aim · Click Shoot / punch
1 / 2 / 3 Fists · pistol · rifle   R Reload on foot · radio in a car
E Talk / interact   M Mission brief
Radio SOLMAR FM 88.5 · COAST 92.1 — press R while driving to switch station
Tab Full map   Esc / P Pause
